What Is a Textile?

Every textile — no matter how technical — comes from the same three-step chain. A fiber gets twisted or spun into a yarn, and that yarn becomes a fabric: woven, knit, or bonded into a nonwoven.

Everything downstream — strength, stretch, moisture management, durability — traces back to choices made at each of these three steps.
