Yarn Construction

Four structural approaches to assembling a yarn. A single is one strand; a plied yarn twists two or more singles together; a core-spun yarn wraps fiber around a spandex core; a drawn covered yarn entangles filament around the core with air.

Each one plays once the first time it scrolls into view — after that, hover a type (or tap it) to replay it on its own.

Covered Yarn — Twist Direction

A covered yarn wraps a cover yarn around an elastic core. Single covered yarn (SCY) has one wrap, in either twist direction — S or Z, named for which way the diagonal of the letter runs. Double covered yarn (DCY) wraps twice: the inner layer goes on Z, then the outer layer wraps S over it, so the two torques cancel.

Plays once on scroll into view; hover a diagram to replay it and hold the S or Z on screen.
