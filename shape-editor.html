Point density (adaptive placement)

…
Points are placed adaptively (worst-residual-first against a dense reference), not uniformly — fewer points where the curve is straight, more where it bends. Moving a slider resamples the curve as it stands right now (hand edits included) at the new count — it does not just add or remove handles at fixed spots. Scrubbing a slider back and forth is lossless; a fresh hand-drag on the canvas resets what "now" means for the next resample.
