- English side: "reduction clearing" searches returned only English-language papers/patents (e.g. Coloration Technology 2016 "The clearing of dyed polyester. Part 1"); no Chinese paper with an English abstract pairing 还原清洗 ↔ reduction clearing was retrieved. 0 bilingual attestations.

**Process-order evidence for polyester/nylon (asked for specifically):**
1. Used after disperse dyeing of polyester/nylon goods: yes, per the 80/20 涤锦 coral-fleece flow above (high-temperature dyeing → 还原清洗 → 水洗 → 中和). That flow is disperse-only; it shows no acid-dye step.
2. A trade source disputes its usefulness for nylon staining: reduction clearing "并不能有效解决锦纶沾色" — the nylon's colour barely changes while the polyester gets paler, attributed to the alkaline bath "剥皮" (peeling) the polyester; the same source says a nylon blocking agent (阻染剂) "对分散染料不起作用". Source: relayed from https://www.cnzcn.net/com/nbyzldhg/news/itemid-592.html / http://www.nizi365.com/article/showw.asp?id=21513 — snippet-only; I could not open either to check the exact sentence.
3. General rule from the 多组分纤维 article (sohu / dye-ol): if the two dye classes need very different conditions or one fibre stains heavily, use the **two-bath method with 水洗、皂洗或还原清洗 between the baths** — i.e. clear the disperse dye BEFORE dyeing the second fibre. Stated for blends in general.
4. For one-bath disperse/reactive (polyester/cotton) the sources say reduction clearing **cannot** be used because it would destroy the other dye on the fabric; only soaping. **No source retrieved states this for disperse + acid on polyester/nylon specifically**, and I found nothing on "酸性还原清洗" for this blend (0 found).
Net: evidence supports "disperse first → reduction clear → then acid-dye the nylon" as the two-bath logic, and gives no support for an alkaline reduction clear AFTER a one-bath disperse+acid dyeing. The polyester/nylon-specific order is **not directly attested**; treat as unconfirmed.

Recommendation: **还原清洗** ; also-known-as: (皂洗 is a different, non-reductive wash — do not use as a synonym) ; confidence: **High** as the Chinese term (5 trade snippets, no competitor), **Low** on the page's process claim for one-bath polyester/nylon.
