# Textile glossary, English → Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans)

**Status: draft for review. No page copy has been translated yet.**

This is the term list for a Chinese version of [`/textile-101`](../../textile-101.html) and
[`/dyeing`](../../dyeing.html). It covers every technical term in the two pages, including the labels drawn
inside the diagrams and the captions the animations write.

- **242 terms**: 196 high confidence, 40 medium, 6 low.
- Section 1 lists only the 46 medium and low terms. A reviewer short on time can read that section alone.
- Section 2 is the full list, grouped the way the pages are.

## Decisions so far

Eva decided six terms on 2026-11-18; they are marked **Decided** in the tables: hemp, air-jet spinning,
ring dyeing, cross dyeing, polyester, polyamide (nylon). Everything else waits for the reviewer.

The English copy was also changed where this glossary found one word doing two jobs ("lap", "ends",
"blended", "clearing rinse"), so rows D-15, G-5, I-19 and I-22 now describe wording that has been made
unambiguous in the source.

## How to review

For each row, the reviewer either accepts the 标准中文 column or writes the term they would use.

- **标准中文** is the term proposed for the page.
- **依据** is the standard or textbook that covers the term (list in section 3).
- **行业常用说法** is what mills and merchandisers say when it differs.
- **置信度**: High = standard term, safe. Medium = a real choice between two terms, or a term that needs a gloss.
  Low = no settled Chinese term that I know of; the proposal is a best guess.

**What the 依据 column does and does not mean.** I could not open the full text of the standards from here.
The column names the standard whose scope covers the term; it does not confirm that the standard prints
exactly this wording. Edition years are given in section 3 only where I could check them. The reviewer, or
anyone with access to 全国标准信息公共服务平台 (std.samr.gov.cn), should confirm wording for any term that matters.

Brand names (莱卡, 天丝) are listed only so they can be avoided.

**Three traps that affect copy, not just single words:**

1. **再生 means both "regenerated" and "recycled".** Section 02 of Textile 101 discusses both. The Chinese copy
   must use 再生纤维素纤维 for viscose and its relatives and 循环再利用 for recycled polymer, and never bare 再生.
2. **"Ends" and "end"/"pick" do not map.** English uses "ends" for yarns fed together and for warp yarns.
   Chinese uses unrelated words, so two sentences need rewriting rather than translating (D-15, F-1).
3. **混纺 or 交织.** "A fabric blended from two fibers" covers both in English. The cross-dyeing figure shows
   polyester one way and nylon the other, which is 交织 (I-22).
