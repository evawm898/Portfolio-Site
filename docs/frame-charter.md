# /frame — charter

The governing document for the Parametric Frame (`frame.html`, `frame.css`, `frame.js`,
`frame-registry.js`, `frame-geometry.js`, `frame-profile-editor.js`). Read it before
touching any frame file. The working agreements of the flower-project skill apply
verbatim (registry as the single source of truth, one owner per boundary, generated by
the shape and never clipped to it, the loudness budget, contact sheets before commits,
numbers not summaries, shipped means reachable); this charter holds what is specific to
the frame and does not restate them.

## 1. What it is

A generator for ornamental frames and Gothic windows as a PRINTABLE RELIEF, with an
engraved, lightly shaded, tattoo-flash drawing of the same frame beside it. The references
are ornate silver mirror frames, oval baroque frames, and Gothic lancet windows with
tracery and ivy.

**Output contract (ruling 1, phase 2 — Eva, Oct 22).** The RELIEF STL is the primary
output and the SVG is the secondary one, the /bug pattern: one model, two readers.
- **STL** (binary, millimetres, `exportStl(relief)` in `frame-relief.js`): the skeleton
  and the molding as a relief — every band's profile `z(x)` lifted over a flat back, the
  lights pierced (default) or backed. Later layers land here first: filigree as relief
  geometry, texture as surface displacement. Hatching is NOT in the STL; in 3D the molding
  shades itself under the one fixed light.
- **SVG** (`exportSvg(model)` in `frame-geometry.js`): layers 1–3, the engraving — the
  swept bands, creases, hatching derived from the profile's normal, cross-hatch. Unchanged
  by phase 2: the default's SVG is byte-identical to `8db018c` (the gate measures it).
- **Both read one model.** `buildFrame` builds the skeleton and the sweep; `buildRelief`
  reads that model and `offsetCurve` and adds nothing to the 2-D geometry; `exportSvg`
  never reads the relief. The 3D view on the page draws the STL's own positions.

**Core model.** Every frame is a PATH (the skeleton) with a MOLDING PROFILE swept along
it. Arch, rectangle and oval are skeleton types feeding the same sweep. The arch is the
open-bottomed / silled case of the one system, never a separate tool.

**Print process is undecided (ruling 3).** Every millimetre floor below is an ASSUMPTION
until a process is chosen and a coupon printed; nothing here has been printed.

## 2. The layer stack

Outer to inner. Settle each before refining the next — a layer is constrained by the one
before it, and ornament tuned against a skeleton that later moves is work done twice.

| # | layer | what it owns | phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Skeleton** | the path: arch (one continuous pointedness; ogee and Tudor later as separate constructions), rectangle, oval; width, height, proportion; mullion count, sill | 1 (arch), 2 (rect/oval) |
| 2 | **Molding** | one 1-D cross-section profile swept along every spine; the draggable profile editor; presets as starting points; width and depth. Grooves are a property of the profile, never an overlay. **In 3D the same profile is the relief** (2): each band's `z(x)` lifted over a flat back | 1, 2 |
| 3 | **Shading** | SVG only: hatching derived from the swept surface's normal against one fixed light; intensity, pitch, line weight — the weight FOLLOWS the darkness (1b), and cross-hatching lands only above a threshold (1b). In 3D the molding shades itself under the same `LIGHT` | 1, 1b |
| 4 | **Filigree / ornament** | RELIEF GEOMETRY grown from named skeleton anchors (apex, springings, sill ends), mirrored, ATTACHED to the molding or the back plate — never floating, never a separate shell with no overlap; a crest/cartouche slot; tracery heads (cusps, trefoil, quatrefoil); density and scale. The SVG draws its projection | 3 |
| 5 | **Texture** | SURFACE DISPLACEMENT on the molding's relief: stone grain, pitting, cracks. Its amplitude has a floor tied to `MIN_FEATURE_MM` (a displacement under the printable feature is noise the slicer cannot resolve); inert at 0, bit-identical. Subtle by default | 4 |
| 6 | **Overgrowth** | ivy and vines along the skeleton edges, drips — relief attached to the molding, generated by the skeleton's edges and never clipped. Sparkles deferred, later shared with /print | 5 |
| 7 | **Interior** | frame mode: empty (pierced), backed (the plate behind the lights), cracked glass, a motif slot | 6 |

## 3. Invariants

1. **One geometry path.** `buildFrame(params, profile)` is the one builder; the drawing
   on screen is `exportSvg(model)`, the same string the download writes. The gate asserts
   this path for path (X1).
2. **The registry is the source of truth.** `frame-registry.js` declares every control,
   section, default, visibility predicate and profile preset. The page generates the
   panel from it and never names a control by hand. Adding a control is one row.
3. **One owner per boundary** (§4). No consumer adds `d · normal` to a spine except
   `offsetCurve`. The gate scans for a second producer (O4) and refuses it.
4. **The shade comes from the geometry.** Darkness is `intensity × (1 − Lambert)` of the
   profile's own normal against `LIGHT`, a fixed vector in the registry. There is no
   light control, no painted gradient and no per-band tint. A flat profile is EXACTLY
   uniform (T1, Tp1); a cove grades (T2, Tp2). **Line weight follows that same darkness**
   (ruling 1, phase 1b): a hatch run's stroke width is `lineWeight × hatchWeight(d)`, one
   law in `frame-geometry.js` reading `HATCH_WEIGHT_RANGE` / `HATCH_WEIGHT_STEPS` from the
   registry, so a flat band is one weight and a graded band several (W1, W2). **Cross-hatch
   is a threshold on the same darkness** (ruling 6): lines across the band only where it
   exceeds `crossHatchThreshold` — none at 1, every band at 0, a band IFF its own darkness
   exceeds it (H1) — and the threshold is an ADVANCED-tier control (U3).
5. **Topology is mode-free and a ladder is derived from a length.** Row counts, sample
   counts and line counts are derived from millimetres (band width over hatch pitch,
   arc step in degrees), never typed as counts.
6. **Clamped and told.** A rise taller than the height, a light too narrow for its
   sub-arch — the geometry holds the value at its floor and `info.clamped` names it; the
   read-out prints it (C1). Never silently stretched, never refused.
7. **Nothing is clipped to the outline.** Hatch lines are generated as offsets of the
   spine they belong to and run out where the tone falls under their threshold; they are
   never drawn across the band and trimmed.
8. **The light and the paper are fixed.** Ink `#0A0A0C` on paper `#EDEDE8`; the page's
   chrome is the site's dark palette around it.
9. **The export is ONE connected watertight solid** (phase 2, from the bloom's own
   invariant). The hard gate is boundary edges = 0 on the welded float32 bytes the STL
   writer wrote (W). Connectedness is VOLUMETRIC: every triangle rasterised into a voxel
   grid at 0.4 mm, 6-connected flood fill, one region; a miss is re-read at 0.2 mm, because
   a real gap survives a smaller cell and a sampling artefact vanishes (C). Both are the
   bug gate's own code, ported. Two dead ends are recorded so nobody re-derives them: a
   vertex-weld shell count is not connectedness (the solid is many closed shells that
   overlap without sharing vertices), and an AABB-overlap graph is worse than no gate
   (boxes overlap when the solids inside them do not). **Overlapping closed shells are
   accepted** — at the apex mitre, at the sub-arch joins, and at every band end, where the
   relief overlaps its neighbour by a declared EMBED (§4) rather than touching it face to
   face. A face-to-face contact reads as one region to the voxel gate and is NOT an
   overlap; the embed is therefore asserted geometrically (B), not by the flood fill.
10. **`MIN_FEATURE_MM` is one constant (1.0 mm, in the registry), labelled an ASSUMPTION
   wherever it is quoted** — here, in the read-out, in the gate — pending the print
   process (ruling 3). It caps the OUTPUT, never the input: a plate thinner than it is
   built at the floor and told; a scaled band or mullion narrower than it is told, not
   refused (`info.underFloor`, F). It is also the embed length (a length derived from a
   length, never a count).

## 4. Boundary ownership map

All in `frame-geometry.js`.

| boundary | owner | consumers (every one) |
|---|---|---|
| every curve parallel to a spine | `offsetCurve(spine, d)` — adds `d · n` per vertex, trims overshoot past a join's bisector | `offsetPoly` (null-free form) → `outerEdge`, `innerEdge`; `sweepSpine` (creases via `offsetPoly`, hatch via `offsetCurve` so vertex indices survive) |
| a band's outer edge / inner edge | `outerEdge(spine, w)` / `innerEdge(spine, w)` | `sweepSpine` (the drawn edges); `archSkeleton` — the sill's top edge (`innerEdge(sill)`) is where every jamb and mullion STARTS; the jambs' inner edges set the lights' clear widths; the head's inner edge caps the sub-arches |
| the join of two arcs at an apex | `joinSpines(a, b)` — the shared vertex carries the MITRE normal | `archSkeleton` (the head, every light) |
| the profile's sampling across the band | `profileSamples(points)` (PCHIP, `PROFILE_SAMPLES`) | `sweepSpine`, `darknessAt`, `creaseIndices`, the editor's drawn curve, `toneAcross` |
| a profile slope → a surface normal | `normalAt(dzds, nrm)` | `darknessAt` |
| a normal → a tone | `lambert(N)` (reads `LIGHT`) | `darknessAt` |
| darkness at a sample | `darknessAt(prof, i, p, nrm)` | `sweepSpine` (the hatch), `toneAcross` (the read-out and the gate) |
| the arch's arcs | `archArcs(a, pointedness)` | `archSkeleton` (the head and every sub-arch — one law) |
| the sub-arch spring line | solved in `archSkeleton` against `innerEdge(head)` by bisection | the mullions' top, every light's arcs |
| the lights' boundaries | `info.lightBounds` — the jambs' `innerEdge` at the two ends (ruling 4), the mullion centrelines between | every sub-arch's half-span and centre |
| a darkness → a stroke width | `hatchWeight(d, lineWeight)` (reads the registry's range and steps) | every hatch run and cross line (`stroke()`) |
| a cross line | `crossHatch` — a CHORD between two of `offsetCurve`'s polylines at the profile samples bracketing the dark stretch, interpolated along the spine; stations every `hatchPitch` mm | `sweepSpine` |
| a band's relief surface (phase 2) | `bandSolid` in `frame-relief.js` — its grid's xy are `offsetCurve(spine, (x_i − 0.5)·w)` at every spine vertex (a `null` past a join's bisector is the join's own mitre point), its z is `plateT + z_i · reliefDepth` | `buildRelief`, the STL, the 3D view; the gate's R reads it back against `offsetCurve` to the double |
| the end EMBED | `spine.embed` — DECLARED by `archSkeleton` (which knows what abuts what) and realised by `embedSpine` in `frame-relief.js`, which extends a COPY of the spine by `MIN_FEATURE_MM` along its end tangent; the sill's outer lip under a stepped sill likewise | `bandSolid` only. `exportSvg` and every 2-D reader never see it (the SVG is byte-identical with the field present) |
| the back plate (backed lights) | `plateOutline` — the frame's outer silhouette read off `outerEdge` of the jambs, the head and the sill (or the step) | `buildRelief` |

The gate's O1–O4 measure this table rather than recite it: jambs and mullions start on
`innerEdge(sill)` to the double; the clear widths are equal measured from the jambs'
inner edges to the mullions' outer edges; sub-arches spring at one height — the outer two
on the jambs' inner edges and the interior ones on the centrelines — and clear
`innerEdge(head)` by one mullion band; and `+ d * spine.nrm` appears once in the source.
H2 asks the same question of every cross line: both ends ON an offset polyline of the band.

## 5. The arch, as built

Half-span `a` is between the jamb CENTRELINES (`width / 2`). `R = a (1 + 2p)`: `p` 0 is
the semicircle, 0.5 the equilateral arch (R = span), 1 the lancet (R = 3a). Both centres
sit on the spring line, `R − a` inside the opposite springing, so the apex is on the
axis at every `p` and the jamb-to-head join is tangent-continuous. `height` is the sill's
underside to the apex; the jambs take what the rise leaves.

Lights: `n` mullions make `n + 1` lights of EQUAL CLEAR WIDTH (jamb inner edge to mullion
outer edge, mullion to mullion). Each light carries a sub-arch of the same pointedness on
its own half-span, springing on the main spring line unless its apex would come within
one mullion band of the head's inner edge, when the sub-spring line drops (ruling 3, 1b:
kept). **The outer sub-arches terminate on the jambs' INNER EDGES** (ruling 4, 1b — read
off `innerEdge(jamb)`, the boundary the clear widths already read; phase 1 ran them to the
jamb centreline, half-buried); the interior joins are mullion centreline to centreline, so
a sub-arch's end is half-buried in the mullion band — accepted. Consequence: the outer
lights' sub-arches are narrower by half a mullion band than the interior ones, so their
apexes sit lower (229.3 against 234.0 mm at the defaults).

The sill is a horizontal spine at `w / 2` whose top edge is what everything stands on;
`stepped` adds a wider, thinner spine below it.

## 6. Shading, as built

The band's across-position `x ∈ [0, 1]` (outer edge to inner). The profile's slope
`dz/ds = (depth / width) · dz/dx` tilts the surface normal about the spine's tangent,
so `N = (−dz/ds · n, 1) / |…|` where `n` is the spine's inward 2-D normal. Darkness
`= intensity · (1 − max(0, N · LIGHT))`. Consequences, all measured by the gate: a flat
band is one normal everywhere, so one tone; a vertical jamb on the left reads
differently from the right under the one light (T3); the same profile on the arch head
grades ALONG the arc as its normal turns.

Hatch: `floor(width / pitch)` candidate lines parallel to the spine. Line `k` is drawn
over the vertices where darkness exceeds a van der Corput threshold `vdc(k)` — evenly
spread thresholds, so the line count is proportional to darkness and each line runs out
exactly where the tone drops under its own threshold. Creases (slope turns > 22°) get a
drawn line; the two edges always do.

**Weight (1b, ruling 1).** Every hatch run is drawn at `lineWeight × (0.5 + d)` for its
darkness `d`, quantised onto six levels; a run SPLITS where its level changes (the boundary
vertex is shared) so a line running from shade into light swells and thins along its
length. The darkest line is three times the lightest; the edges stay at 1.4 × nominal.
Default intensity is **1.00** (ruling 1). Measured on the cove's left jamb through the
close camera, outer → inner bins of pixel ink: **0.235 / 0.235 / 0.118** against phase 1's
0.176 / 0.118 / 0.118 at intensity 0.65 — the outer gradient reads as two lines' worth of
ink where it read as one, and the weights on that jamb run 0.30–0.57 mm at the 0.40
nominal. The SVG carries one `<path>` per distinct width per band (W2), so a 9-band frame
is a few dozen paths rather than hundreds.

**Cross-hatch (1b, ruling 6).** Where the darkness exceeds `crossHatchThreshold`
(default **0.70**, Advanced tier), a line is laid ACROSS the band every `hatchPitch` mm
along the spine, covering exactly the profile samples above the threshold — a deep cove,
a shadowed step, a reed's shaded wall. It is a chord between two `offsetCurve` polylines
(H2), never a second producer, and it runs out where the tone drops. At 0.70 the shipped
default cross-hatches 6 of 9 bands (366 lines); the reeded and fluted presets, whose
walls reach darkness 1.00, carry thousands (6,750 / 4,084) — the molding is the loudest
element there, which ruling 2 says it must not be once filigree lands, and the sheet
photographs it for the ruling. Perpendicular rather than at 45°, and at the hatch pitch
rather than a pitch of its own: both are decisions without a ruling (§9).

## 8. Verification plan

Per phase, the gate is `node tools/verify-frame.mjs` (part one Node in seconds, part two
Chromium in ~40 s; `--negative-control` runs the mutants, part one only, and says so;
`--no-browser` for iteration) and the sheet is `node tools/shot-frame-sheet.mjs <dir>`.
**The gate runs in CI** (`.github/workflows/frame.yml`, since 1b): the full gate and then
the negative control, on any PR touching the frame files, the two frame tools, the charter
or the workflow. **A frame-only PR no longer runs the flower gates**: the two that carried
`'tools/**'` (`flower-export-watertight`, `flower-geometry-quality`) are narrowed to the
files they reach, proved by a picomatch simulation of every workflow's `paths` over a
frame-only, a cards-only and a flower diff (frame → `frame.yml` alone; flower-geometry.js
→ the five flower workflows; before: both flower gates on every frame and cards diff).

Every phase owes:
- a byte comparison against a worktree of the base commit for the rows the change
  should not move (`exportSvg` strings are the bytes here);
- a clause in the O family for every new boundary, with a mutant that restates it;
- a tone measurement on pixels through a close camera, not the whole-frame view (1.3
  px/mm, where a 0.4 mm line is grey);
- a contact sheet at one pinned viewBox, captions carrying the read-out's numbers, no
  pixel delta quoted (the renderer is deterministic here — V0 reads 0 px twice — but a
  sheet is for ruling by eye).

Phase 1's measured figures are in the session report and in the gate's own output.

**Phase 2's gate is `node tools/verify-frame-relief.mjs`** (Node only, seconds; the STL
needs no browser): W boundary edges = 0 · C one region at 0.4 mm, re-read at 0.2 on a
miss · R registration — relief z at every grid vertex is `plateT + z_i · reliefDepth` and
xy is `offsetCurve` to the double on every original spine vertex · L a pierced light's
clear width in the STL equals the 2-D clear width to 1e-6 mm · B every declared embed
lands inside the band it names · F the floor flags both ways. Rows: the shipped DEFAULT
first (the bloom shipped a multi-piece default for months because no row exercised it),
every profile preset, 0 and 4 mullions, each sill, pierced and backed.
`--negative-control` runs its mutants (an open edge, a detached mullion, a light offset
from `offsetCurve`, an embed removed). It rides in `frame.yml` beside the 2-D gate. The
3D sheet is `node tools/shot-frame-relief.mjs <dir>`: a fixed camera, chrome hidden, and
close-ups of the cove and the apex mitre.

## 9. Decisions made without a ruling (phase 1)

Each names what was rejected and how hard it is to undo.

1. **Mullions 0–4, not 1–4.** 0 is the plain arch, and it is the flat-band tone fixture.
   *Undo:* one `min` in the registry.
2. **Pointedness is `R = a(1 + 2p)`**, 0.5 = equilateral. The alternative, centres at a
   fraction of the span, has no named midpoint. *Undo:* `archArcs`.
3. **Light `(−0.45, 0.55, 0.70)`**, upper-left. *Undo:* `LIGHT`, one vector.
4. **The hatch is parallel to the spine, not across it.** Across-hatching the band would
   put the engraving's lines against the molding's grain. *Undo:* `sweepSpine`.
5. **Default preset ogee, pitch 1.1 mm, weight 0.4 mm.** From the sheet; **ogee and
   intensity 1.00 are RULED now** (1b, rulings 1 and 2). *Undo:* registry defaults.
6. **Sub-arches spring on the main spring line and drop only when the head forces it.**
   RULED kept (1b, ruling 3). *Undo:* the `clearanceAt` solve.
7. **`profilePreset` reads `custom` on the first drag and never snaps back.** *Undo:*
   `markCustom` in `frame.js`.

Phase 1b added, each without a ruling:

8. **`HATCH_WEIGHT_RANGE = [0.5, 1.5]`, six levels.** A 3:1 swell at the pitch the hatch
   already has; wider and the heavy lines touch at pitch 1.1 mm. *Undo:* two constants.
9. **Cross-hatch threshold default 0.70.** Chosen so the shipped ogee crosses only on its
   deep side (max 0.74 on the right jamb) and the cove's outer bin (0.706) just crosses,
   while the flat band (0.30) and the light side never do. The cove's outer stretch is
   ONE sample wide at that value, so its cross lines are dots. *Undo:* one default.
10. **Cross lines are perpendicular to the spine and at the hatch pitch.** A 45° set and
   an independent pitch were both considered; perpendicular is one scan direction and no
   second constant. On a narrow dark stretch (the ogee's right jamb, three samples) it
   reads as a rail of ticks rather than a tone — photographed on the sheet's last cell.
   *Undo:* `crossHatch`'s endpoints and step.
11. **The Advanced tier is one toggle button, hiding nothing from Standard.** Registry
   `tier` field, `applyVisibility` reads it. *Undo:* `setAdvanced`.
12. **Proportions (ruling 5) kept as shipped** — the brief left that line blank.

Phase 2 added, each without a ruling (ruling 2 parks items 3, 8–11 as SVG-only until
3D exists; they are unchanged):

13. **`printHeight` default 180 mm** — the overall size, the model's height scaled to it
    (the SLS dyed-PA12 shortest-axis cap, the one printing number this repo carries; a
    guess until ruling 3 lands). *Undo:* one default.
14. **`plateThickness` default 2.0 mm, `reliefScale` 1.0, lights `pierced`.** *Undo:*
    three defaults.
15. **The embed is `MIN_FEATURE_MM` long**, at both ends of every standing or arched
    band and under a stepped sill — one length, never a per-band number. *Undo:* `EMBED_MM`.
16. **The 3D view carries no hatching**: the relief under one light IS the shading there,
    and a hatched texture on a surface already shaded by its own normal would be the same
    information twice. *Undo:* a second material.
17. **Relief depth is `moldingDepth × scale × reliefScale`** — the profile's own depth
    control, scaled with the print, times one multiplier; not a separate millimetre
    control, so the 2-D shading and the 3-D relief describe one profile. *Undo:* `reliefDepthMm`.
