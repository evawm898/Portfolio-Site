# Textile 101 accuracy sheet: claim → source → status

Covers every factual claim in sections 02–08 as rebuilt, plus section 09's claims as kept.

**How to read the status column**

- **Sourced** — a named source gives the figure or statement.
- **Sourced (weak)** — the only sources found were trade sites, vendor pages or an encyclopedia.
- **Unsourced** — standard textbook knowledge that this run could not tie to a retrieved source.
- **Eva to verify** — listed again at the end with the specific question.

**A caveat on every source below.** The fact-check ran by web search only; page fetches were blocked in this environment. Each "source gives" is the search-result excerpt, not the page read in full. ISO 2076, the BISFA terminology booklet and the ASTM D1909 table were not seen at all.

## Eva to verify

1. **Lyocell moisture regain, 11–13 %.** Only blog-level sources. Is there a maker's figure you trust?
2. **Covered-yarn card.** "Stretched 3×, the core adds about 13 D → roughly 85 D in all." Is 3× a fair worked example, and is it acceptable to ignore the cover yarn's take-up?
3. **The two specs I replaced.** `2/78/68 Crimp Polyamide` was decoded as 78 *denier*; I rewrote it as 78 *dtex*. `1/8/36 Recycled Polyester` (8 denier, 36 filaments) looked like a typo and was dropped. What were the real counts?
4. **"DTY … the workhorse of knitted performance wear."** Your call on the phrasing.
5. **Cotton is matte "because the uneven surface scatters light".** Reasoned from the mercerisation source, not stated by one.
6. **Viscose "sets with a serrated outline".** True as a description; the mechanism is not on the page because I could not source it.
7. **Hollow = "bulk and insulation for less weight — fiberfill, thermal layers".** The only source found is a carpet-fibre patent.
8. **Silk "like a prism".** A common phrasing; the source is an encyclopedia.
9. **The Scardino chart.** The four group lists are yours from before; I reworded the glosses and added the framing sentence. Check the wording of "High modulus", "Stitchbonded" and "Biaxial bonded" against the original figure.
10. **Draft convention.** Filled = warp on top. It matches `/weave`. Is it the convention you want taught?
11. **The panel's teal.** The taxonomy panel draws in `#5FA0A0`; the new figures and the knit panels draw in the petrol token `#2FA3A3`. See decision D3.
