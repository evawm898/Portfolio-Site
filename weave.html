Structure
Threading

one shaft per end: click a cell to thread the end there, click it again to unthread.

Tie-up & treadling

tie-up marks say which shafts RISE on a treadle. a pick may press more than one treadle.

Yarns

the library every end and pick draws from. count is stored as denier whatever unit you type it in; a yarn in use cannot be removed.

Systems
warp systems
weft systems

a system is a name and a repeat of yarn × count. with several systems the threads cycle through them in order, taking each system's "in turn" count before moving on.
