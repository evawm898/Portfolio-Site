Marble

Suminagashi and ebru, by the equations — every drop, tine, comb and stir is a closed-form deformation of the sheet, and the link is the sheet.

Tool

View & layers

bath view · layer 1 of 4 — work in the bath; lay paper to see the print, with its paper, mottle and flaws; a pull freezes the bath as a printed layer and the next bath multiplies over it.

Colour
Ink
This ink

Kept per colour and written into every drop of it. Concentration reads full until the ink spreads — a region twice its dropped area reads half. Spread (gall) pushes wider from the same ink, so it drops paler.

Sheet
Material

Shown on the paper view and in the PNG: the paper's texture, a faint mottle inside the ink, each ink's granulation, hairlines breaking into dashes, and the pull's skips, air bubbles, uneven edge and touch-down line — all seeded, so the link reproduces them. The SVG stays clean vector.

Settings
Patterns

Classic sequences scripted from the current colour: concentric drops; a stone base raked back and forth (get-gel); a fine comb once through it (nonpareil) and back again (chevron); a wide comb drawn wavy through it (bouquet).
