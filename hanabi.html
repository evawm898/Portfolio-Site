EM / HANABI

Hanabi deck

Five suits of fireworks, ranks one to five — every suit carries a symbol and its own firework as well as a colour, so the deck reads in greyscale. One corner index for the whole deck; the Fireworks board designs the art of every suit × rank; then a size and a back, and an imposed print PDF or 300 dpi PNGs.

01Suits

Each suit picks how its rank is shown (a count of fireworks by default, or one firework growing with rank), which firework type it draws, and under Scale an optional second tier at ranks 4 and 5. The Fireworks board edits every card.

06Firework spec read only

Each card's centre art is drawn from a spec: the type's defaults, then the suit's parameters, then the card's own. The Fireworks board edits them; this shows the merged spec for one card.
