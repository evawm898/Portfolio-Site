#textile-circuit-construction

Three Ways to Build a Textile Circuit

Three approaches to putting a conductive pathway into fabric, each starting from the same plain swatch with a connection point on either side. Plays once on scroll into view — hover a swatch to replay it. (Three more — conductive printed ink, laminated flexible trace, and embedded wire — get their own closer look below.)

01
Conductive yarn
Integrated into the textile structure
Yarn enters → loops or interlaces → pathway is formed
02
Metal-coated fabric
Fibers or fabric are coated with a conductive metal
Fiber is coated → coating spreads → conductive area emerges
03
Sewn conductive trace
Conductive thread is stitched through the textile
Needle penetrates → thread loops through → stitched pathway grows
