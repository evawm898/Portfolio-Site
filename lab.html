#textile-circuit-construction

Six Ways to Build a Textile Circuit

Six approaches to putting a conductive pathway into fabric, each starting from the same plain swatch with a connection point on either side. Plays once on scroll into view — hover a swatch to replay it.

01
Conductive yarn
Integrated into the textile structure
Yarn enters → loops or interlaces → pathway is formed
02
Printed conductive ink
Deposited onto the textile surface
Ink is deposited → line takes shape → trace is completed
03
Metal-coated fabric
Fibers or fabric are coated with a conductive metal
Fiber is coated → coating spreads → conductive area emerges
04
Sewn conductive trace
Conductive thread is stitched through the textile
Needle penetrates → thread loops through → stitched pathway grows
05
Embedded wire
An insulated wire is routed through the textile
Channel opens → wire feeds through → textile encloses it
06
Laminated conductive trace
A thin conductive layer is bonded to the textile
Layers assemble → heat and pressure bond them → trace is enclosed
More components land here as they're built.
